National Stories & Trends

Louisville's staggering second half: No. 11 Louisville beat Morehead State 107–57. Freshman Obinna Ekezie Jr. posted 20 points and 13 rebounds off the bench. The Cardinals outscored the Eagles 40–2 during the first 12:33 of the second half, including 33 consecutive points. Louisville held a 59–23 rebound advantage and scored 56 bench points. It follows an 84–70 opener against Chattanooga. AP recap.
