Alabama First: No. 18 Tide 109, Oakland 68

Alabama improved to 3–0, led 56–29 at halftime and drilled 24 of 46 three-pointers (52.2%), one short of its school record. London Jemison scored 20 on 7-of-7 shooting, including a perfect 6-of-6 from deep. Amari Allen played after his reported ankle sprain and delivered 14 points, six assists and zero turnovers. Aden Holloway returned for his season debut and started, scoring 12 on four triples. The team tied its school record with 30 assists. However, Oakland won the rebounding battle 37–31, including 17 offensive rebounds according to CBS; that is a concern against better teams.
