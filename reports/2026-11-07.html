ALABAMA FIRST · No. 20 Alabama 101, Arkansas State 86

Amari Allen: 16 points, 13 rebounds, 11 assists — fifth triple-double in Alabama men's basketball history. Alabama recovered from an early 19–11 deficit, led 51–34 at halftime and improved to 2–0. Qayden Samuels scored 17, Drew Fielder 16 and Brandon Garrison posted 14 points and 10 rebounds.

Starting five: Qayden Samuels, Amari Allen, Drew Fielder, Brandon Garrison, London Jemison. Scoring: starters 71, bench 30. Team: 27/76 FG, 15/50 three, 32/43 FT; 53 rebounds, 19 assists, 14 turnovers. Arkansas State: 32/76 FG, 15/39 three, 7/16 FT; 42 rebounds.

Injury watch: Allen landed awkwardly and grabbed his right ankle late, needed help off court, then returned to the bench. No definitive medical diagnosis was reported in the verified recap. Jaxon Richardson missed the game with an upper-body injury. Aden Holloway completed his five-game suspension; confirm status before next game.

Valparaiso won in West Lafayette after two overtimes. Purdue dropped to 0–2, an early-season warning sign; its last 0–2 opening was reported as 1976. This is a result, not a projection.

No. 4 UConn 85, No. 16 Michigan 77

UConn avenged its 2026 national championship defeat. Braylon Mullins scored 23 and Silas Demary Jr. added 21, seven rebounds and four assists; Michigan's Trey McKenney scored 22. UConn's 43–30 halftime lead held up.

Saint Louis 90, Missouri 84

Saint Louis improved to 2–0 and Missouri fell to 1–1 in a noteworthy SEC–Atlantic 10 result. Follow up on the Billikens' next high-major test.
