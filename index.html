2. Around the Country

No. 8 Houston 92, No. 11 Kansas 83: Houston won the Players Era 8 championship, racing to a 54–34 halftime lead. Mercy Miller scored 21, Delrecco Gillespie 19 and Dedan Thomas Jr. 16 with eight assists. Kansas freshman Tyran Stokes returned from a bone bruise to record 22 points and 11 rebounds. AP recap.

Texas A&M 97, TCU 92 (OT): the Aggies rallied from a 12-point halftime deficit to improve to 6–0. PJ Haggerty scored 29 and Jalen Shelley 19, eight in overtime. AP recap.

Oregon State 82, Oregon 71: the Beavers moved to 6–0; VCU 83, Santa Clara 77 (OT) and Southern Miss 103, Grambling 100 (2OT) were other notable finishes on the CBS slate.

3. SEC Watch & Losses

SEC LOSS — Auburn 73, Notre Dame 78. Auburn (3–3) fell in the Players Era 8 fifth-place game despite Tahaad Pettiford and Owen Freeman scoring 13 each. The Tigers tied the game repeatedly but missed six straight late free throws; Braeden Smith scored 23 for Notre Dame and hit a decisive late basket. This was Auburn's third loss through six games. Official Auburn recap.

Florida 85, West Virginia 72. Florida bounced back from Wednesday's 86–76 loss to Houston, improving to 6–1. The 13-point response was significant after its first loss; individual player numbers await the official box score. CBS final.

Texas A&M 97, TCU 92 (OT). The Aggies remained unbeaten at 6–0 after trailing 51–39 at halftime. PJ Haggerty's 29 points and Jalen Shelley's late three helped secure the road win. The comeback ended a four-game losing streak against TCU. AP recap.

No. 6 Texas 92, Southern Utah 68. Texas improved to 5–0 with a comfortable 24-point home win; the CBS scoreboard confirms the result, but official player statistics and game-flow details remain pending. CBS final.

SEC roster news — LSU: RJ Luis Jr. is stepping away from the program amid an eligibility dispute; see Health & Roster Watch for the confirmed update. Reuters.

4. Big Stories & Season Trends

Houston's early résumé is strengthening: after beating No. 1 Florida 86–76 on Wednesday, Houston defeated No. 11 Kansas 92–83 Thursday to win the Players Era 8 tournament and move to 6–0. AP.

Texas A&M's perfect start survived its first road test: the Aggies are 6–0 after an overtime rally at TCU, their best opening since 2017–18. AP.

East Texas A&M's early surprise run has cooled: after road wins over Oklahoma and Rice earlier this month, the Lions lost 86–54 at California on Thursday and now stand 3–3. CBS.
