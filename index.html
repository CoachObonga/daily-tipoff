Big Stories and Rolling Trends

East Texas A&M's 2–0 road surprise becomes 2–1

The Lions followed their wins at Oklahoma (68–64, Nov. 2) and Rice (72–71, Nov. 5) with a home overtime loss to Fairleigh Dickinson, 80–76. Alonzo Dodd had 25 points, eight rebounds and five assists, but FDU's David Jevtic scored 21 and Bol Agu 18. This reverses the unbeaten-start narrative and merits monitoring in the Lions' next road game at Portland on November 13.

Michigan rebounds from UConn setback

No. 20 Michigan beat Marquette 84–70 after its earlier loss to UConn. J.P. Estrella scored 18, Quinn Costello made five threes, and Elliot Cadeau had 10 assists. Michigan's 11-for-27 perimeter shooting was a marked positive.

St. John's stays unbeaten

No. 13 St. John's moved to 3–0 with a 90–59 victory over Colgate; Ian Jackson and Keyshawn Hall each scored 17, while Quinn Ellis recorded nine assists. The Johnnies have won all three games by double digits.
